Pick the adventure camp if you want to spend the afternoon doing things rather than sitting around. The resort's rope course has roughly ten activities, and the package also includes cycling, a short trek and a full set of games, from sand volleyball to carrom. After dark the pace drops: music, a bonfire and, on clear nights, a film shown in the open.

Groups of friends, families and office teams get the most out of it. You sleep in two-person tents, and tea, dinner and breakfast are included. Getting there is up to you: about two hours by road from Hyderabad, or a train or bus to Vikarabad.
