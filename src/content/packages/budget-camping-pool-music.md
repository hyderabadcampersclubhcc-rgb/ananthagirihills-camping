This is the most affordable of the four Ananthagiri campsites, and it packs a lot into one night. You arrive at 4 pm, swim until 10, play box cricket and other games late into the night, sit around the campfire and eat a hot buffet of bagara rice and curries. On Saturdays a DJ plays from 8 pm. In the morning there is a South Indian breakfast before you leave at 11.

It suits solo travellers, couples, families and groups of friends alike. The campsite is about 9 km from Vikarabad station, so you can drive, or take an afternoon train from Nampally or Secunderabad and finish the trip by auto.
